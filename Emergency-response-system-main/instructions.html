Safety Instructions

Click a category to expand step-by-step guidance for that type of emergency.

🔥 Fire ▾
  1. Stay calm.
  2. Raise the alarm so others nearby are aware.
  3. Leave the building using the safest available exit.
  4. Do not use elevators during a fire.
  5. Move to a safe location, away from the building.
  6. Call emergency services once you are safe.
🚑 Medical Emergency ▾
  1. Check whether the person is responsive.
  2. Call for medical assistance immediately.
  3. Keep the person safe and comfortable while waiting for help.
  4. Do not move an injured person unnecessarily.
  5. Stay with them and monitor their breathing until help arrives.
🚗 Accident ▾
  1. Check for immediate danger before approaching (traffic, fire, fuel leaks).
  2. Turn on hazard lights and warn oncoming traffic if it's a road accident.
  3. Call emergency services and describe the location clearly.
  4. Do not move seriously injured people unless there's an immediate danger.
  5. Provide basic first aid only if you are trained to do so.
🚨 Crime ▾
  1. Prioritize your personal safety — move away from danger if possible.
  2. Do not confront a suspect directly.
  3. Contact local authorities as soon as it is safe to do so.
  4. Note details that may help authorities (description, location, direction of travel).
  5. Avoid disturbing the scene if a crime has already occurred.
🌪️ Natural Disaster ▾
  1. Follow official evacuation orders and local authority guidance.
  2. Move to higher ground or a designated safe shelter as appropriate for the event.
  3. Avoid flooded roads, damaged structures, and downed power lines.
  4. Keep a battery-powered radio or phone charged for updates.
  5. Check in with family and report your status once you are safe.

In immediate danger? This platform does not dispatch emergency services automatically. Always contact your local emergency number directly.
