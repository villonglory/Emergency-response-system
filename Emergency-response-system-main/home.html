How it works

Built for fast, clear emergency reporting

ERS gives you a straightforward way to report an emergency, share exactly where you are, and know what to do next.

📝

Report Instantly

Fill out a short form describing the emergency and submit it in seconds — no account required.

📍

Share Your Location

Use one tap to capture your GPS coordinates so responders know exactly where to look.

📋

Track With a Report ID

Every report gets a unique ERS ID and a live status: Pending, Responding, or Resolved.

About ERS

A focused tool for emergency reporting and response tracking

The Emergency Response System is a web-based platform designed to help users report emergencies quickly and provide essential information that can support emergency response and personal safety.

  • ✓ Fast emergency reporting
  • ✓ Location sharing
  • ✓ Safety information
  • ✓ Organized emergency records
  • ✓ Administrator monitoring
ℹ️

Important note

This is a portfolio/demonstration project. It does not automatically contact police, ambulance, or fire services. In a real emergency, always call your local emergency number directly.
